        If something goes wrong

        999 / 112

        Ask for Police, then Mountain Rescue. Give your location, what happened and how many people are with you.

        Mobile signal is patchy in Glen Affric. Register for UK emergency SMS before the trip: text register to 999 and follow the reply. It only works on a UK network, so it may not work on a Dutch SIM while roaming; check with your provider.

        • Leave your route and expected finish time with someone at home.
        • Alltbeithe hostel and Camban bothy are the only shelter between Loch Affric and Morvich.
        • If a river is too high to cross safely, wait or turn back. Spate rivers drop fast once rain stops.
        Distances are approximate. Bus and train times change; check Citylink and ScotRail a few weeks before.
